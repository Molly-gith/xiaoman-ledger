# 小满 / Xiaoman
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## 产品 / Product
为有工资收入的普通职场人提供工资周期记账，先安排储蓄和账单，再了解还能安心花多少。沿用已批准的 PRD、周期和财务公式。
Salary-cycle bookkeeping for salaried adults. Plan savings and bills, then understand remaining everyday money. The approved PRD and financial rules remain authoritative.

## 约束 / Constraints
- Alpha 本机存储；保留旧账、备份、手动 CRUD、可确认的消费性质。
- 工资建议：100% 可用收入、15% 计划储蓄、35% 账单预留，剩余用于日常。2026-09-15 用户批准；属于小满的可编辑起点，非书中原比例。
- Local Alpha; preserve storage compatibility, backups, manual CRUD and user-confirmed expense nature.
- User-approved starter: income 100%, savings 15%, bills 35%, remainder everyday. Editable product preset, not a quotation from the book.

## 品牌方向 / Brand commitments
用户要求原创卡通、童话感、乐园感、治愈感和动画电影质感。森林场景作为本轮可调整的执行选择。中文优先，交付说明中英双语。复杂性由 AI 承担。
Original cartoon, storybook, park-like, comforting animated-film quality. Forest setting is an inferred implementation choice for this iteration. Chinese-first interface; bilingual delivery. AI owns technical complexity.

## Evidence
用户提供的三张截图：支出来源难理解、语音按钮没有语音形态、预算缺少工资驱动建议。
Three user screenshots identify unclear allocation, a weak voice affordance and missing salary-based defaults.
